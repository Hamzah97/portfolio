About

Field robotics systems builder

I am a mechatronics engineer with over five years of experience spanning tactical UAV operations, field deployment, and needs-based R&D for military autonomous systems. My core strength lies in end-to-end robotic system design — from mechanical payload engineering to software integration and field testing.

At the Jordanian Armed Forces Drones Department, I lead the integration and operational deployment of advanced tactical UAS platforms, including Quantum Systems Vector and AeroVironment PUMA 3 AE. I also develop custom solutions for operational needs, such as engineering a real-time KLV/MISB metadata parser that transforms live drone video telemetry into precise UTM target coordinates — an effort recognized by a special award from the Army Commander.

Beyond aerial platforms, my M.Sc. thesis demonstrated a pneumatic detect-to-extract demining payload integrated with a Clearpath Jackal UGV. The system utilized RTK/IMU navigation and successfully executed six of eight extraction trials on inert decoys in pilot field deployments. My long-term vision is to design and deploy autonomous robotic systems — aerial and ground — for real-world field applications like infrastructure inspection and agricultural monitoring, closing the gap between controlled-lab prototypes and robust field systems.
